PsychCare Official Clinical Policy

Privacy Policy for PsychCare

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 • Application: PsychCare (com.psychcare.clinical)

1. Introduction

PsychCare ("we", "our", or "us") provides clinical practice management and therapeutic documentation software designed specifically for licensed psychologists, therapists, and healthcare professionals. We are deeply committed to patient confidentiality, clinical privacy, and data security.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, stored, and protected when you use the PsychCare mobile application, web application, and related services.

2. Information We Collect

A. Practitioner Account Details

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Practice Information (Optional): Clinic name, specialization, and state/national license numbers for professional letterhead formatting.
  • Subscription Tier: Current account tier (Trial, Free, or Premium Pro) and subscription renewal timestamps.

B. Clinical Practice & Patient Records

When practitioners use PsychCare to maintain clinical workflow records, the following information may be recorded:

  • Patient Profiles: Demographics, emergency contacts, clinical risk levels (Low, Medium, High, Severe), and primary DSM-5/ICD-11 diagnostic classifications.
  • SOAP Session Notes: Subjective reports, Objective observations, Diagnostic assessments, and Treatment plans.
  • Psychometric Outcome Tests: Standardized responses and severity metrics for PHQ-9 (Depression) and GAD-7 (Anxiety) tests.
  • Appointment Calendar: Dates, times, durations, and session formats (In-Person or Telehealth).

3. Offline Storage & Confidentiality

  • Local Offline Storage: All patient records, clinical session notes, psychometric tests, and appointments are stored securely on the practitioner's local device.
  • Strict Practitioner Isolation: Each psychologist's data is strictly isolated to their login session. Platform administrators have no visibility or access to the private patient files or notes of other practitioners.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize patient or practitioner data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

4. Advertising & Tracking

  • Ad-Free Experience: PsychCare does not display advertisements.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not utilize third-party behavioral trackers or ad-targeting analytics SDKs.

5. Data Ownership and Deletion

Practitioners retain 100% ownership of their data. Individual patient records, notes, assessments, and appointments can be deleted at any time directly through the application. Deleting a patient profile automatically deletes all associated session records from the database.

6. Children's Privacy

PsychCare is a professional clinical tool intended for adult practitioners (aged 18 and older). We do not directly collect information from children. Any therapeutic records involving minor patients are managed solely under the professional clinical care of the licensed practitioner.
