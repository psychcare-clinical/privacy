## 1. Introduction

PsychCare ("we", "our", or "us") provides a specialized clinical practice management and therapeutic documentation application designed for licensed psychologists, therapists, and mental health practitioners. 

We take clinical privacy, patient confidentiality, and data protection with the utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the PsychCare mobile application, web application, and related services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Practitioner Account Information
When you register and use PsychCare as a practitioner or administrator, we may collect:
* **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, and authentication credentials.
* **Professional Information (Optional):** Clinic or practice name, specialization, and clinical license number (used for professional header formatting on exported clinical documentation).
* **Subscription & Licensing Data:** Trial status, license tier (Free, Trial, Premium Pro), and renewal/expiration timestamps.

### B. Clinical Practice & Patient Data (Locally Hosted)
As a practitioner using PsychCare to document your therapeutic practice, you may enter:
* **Client / Patient Profiles:** Names, demographic details, contact phone numbers, emergency contact information, risk assessment indicators (Low, Medium, High, Severe), and primary DSM-5/ICD-11 diagnostic classifications.
* **SOAP Session Notes:** Subjective observations, Objective clinical findings, Diagnostic Assessments, and Treatment Plans.
* **Psychometric Assessment Data:** Questionnaire responses and computed scores for standardized clinical batteries (such as PHQ-9 for Depression and GAD-7 for Anxiety).
* **Clinical Appointment Schedules:** Date, time, duration, modality (In-Person, Telehealth), and status of patient appointments.

---

## 3. How We Store and Protect Clinical Data

### A. Local Storage & Clinical Confidentiality
* **Offline-First Storage:** All client clinical records, SOAP session notes, psychometric evaluations, and appointment schedules are stored securely on your local device.
* **No Sale or Brokerage of Data:** We **never** sell, rent, monetize, or disclose patient medical records, clinical notes, or practitioner data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
* **Strict Role-Based Segregation:** Each practitioner's data is isolated to their specific account. Platform administrators cannot inspect, view, or export the confidential patient notes, diagnostic tests, or clinical records of practicing psychologists.

### B. PDF Export & Device Sharing
* PsychCare provides functionality allowing practitioners to generate confidential PDF reports for SOAP notes and psychometric outcomes.
* Generated PDFs are saved to temporary sandboxed local storage on the device and shared strictly through the native operating system's share sheet (e.g., Apple AirDrop, Android Share, local file save) at the practitioner's explicit direction.

---

## 4. Advertising & Tracking

* **No Advertisements:** PsychCare is an ad-free clinical software tool. We do not display third-party advertisements.
* **No Tracking SDKs:** We do not embed behavioral tracking SDKs or cross-app analytics trackers that monitor user behavior across third-party websites or services.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Practitioner Control:** You retain full ownership and control over your clinical data at all times.
* **Client & Record Deletion:** You can delete individual client profiles, session notes, assessments, or appointments directly within the app. Deleting a client profile cascades to delete all associated notes and evaluations from the local database.
* **Account Removal:** Practitioners can request full account deletion, which permanently erases their profile and linked administrative records.

---

## 6. Permissions Requested by the Mobile Application

The PsychCare Android application may request the following system permissions solely to perform core functionality:
* **Storage / Filesystem Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on supported Android versions):** To save exported PDF clinical summaries and assessment outcome charts to your device storage.
* **Audio Recording (`RECORD_AUDIO` - Optional):** Used exclusively when you choose to activate the voice dictation tool to record clinical session notes. Audio is processed on-device for transcription and is never uploaded to external advertising servers.
* **Internet (`INTERNET`):** Used for account synchronization, authentication verification, and subscription status updates.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

PsychCare is a professional practice management tool intended exclusively for adult licensed mental health practitioners (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. When practitioners record data regarding pediatric or adolescent clients, such records are maintained under the practitioner's sole clinical custody in accordance with applicable healthcare regulations.

## 8. Compliance with Healthcare Privacy Standards

PsychCare is engineered with principles reflecting major healthcare data privacy frameworks:
* **HIPAA Compliance Readiness:** Clinical documentation is structured with standard security safeguards, access controls, and strict practitioner isolation.
* **GDPR & International Privacy Rights:** Users in applicable jurisdictions possess rights to access, rectify, restrict, and erase their personal data.
